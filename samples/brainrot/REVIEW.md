# Brainrot ads, first live samples (2026-11-07)

These are three deliberately terrible AI meme ads, one per sponsor. Each opens on an outrageous
stunt, uses original non-human characters, and ends with the product's real facts and its site.
`tests/brainrot.live.test.ts` made them from each campaign's product brief, which it reads from
`data/nomen.db` without writing to it. Nothing was posted. The format is described in
[docs/brainrot.md](../../docs/brainrot.md).

- `*-brainrot.mp4`: compressed 540 px copies. The 1080x1920 originals are in
  `data/media/brainrot-live/<name>-v2/` on the machine that made them.
- `*-frames.jpg`: one frame a second. Agents that can't play video should look at these.

### monid.ai: the anthem

**Built on** [@br.ai.nrot, "Tralalero Tralala - The Italian Brainrot Song 2"](https://www.tiktok.com/@br.ai.nrot/video/7495815620152364310):
44,410,858 views, 3,492,669 likes, 1,509,630 shares and 641,602 saves on an account with 152,395
followers (291x). It is 78 s and was posted 2025-04-21.

**The video:** a tuxedo penguin, "Monidino Saldolino", jet-skis off a ramp into a pile of gold coins
under "POV: your agent has one balance". Then each creature carries one real feature:
- a crab drowning in API keys ("Keyo Keyo Keyo Subscribini, sign-ups made him cry-ini");
- an owl ranking "twelve picks by price-o";
- a cheetah through an open turnstile ("no sign-up, just go-ino");
- a piggy-bank hippo ("pay per call, that's all-o");
- an octopus with "twenty-nine hundred tools".

The gang ends on "we killed subscriptions, bambino". Each line is a karaoke lyric with its keyword
coloured, and each shot gets a stamp: +1000 AURA, IT'S OVER, CHAT IS THIS REAL, SIGMA, NO
SUBSCRIPTIONS, MOGGED, WE'RE SO BACK. The end card is the penguin with "monid.ai" and "$1 FREE
CREDIT". Every claim is from monid.ai.

**Weak spots:**
- The five middle creatures are a row of parallel gags, so the middle gets samey.
- The hippo shot has a human hand dropping the coin.
- The owl-dog hybrid looks muddled.
- The check called the bare-chested cheetah "slightly suggestive". It is a stylised animal, not
  sexual, but worth a look before it goes on a sponsor's account.

### usenaive.ai: the soap opera

**Built on** [@mommyyame1, "p: 1 #fruitdrama"](https://www.tiktok.com/@mommyyame1/video/7649489303176809741):
81,058,835 views, 4,174,069 likes, 346,334 shares and 247,587 saves on an account with 234,764
followers (345x). It is 101 s and was posted 2026-06-09.

**The video** goes one huge word at a time, read by a deep story voice, over sad piano that turns
to phonk at the twist:
- Gary the potato drifts a shopping cart down a gravy river in front of a fireball ("POV: you
  can't afford a team").
- He can't do all the work (COOKED), and Baron Glaze, a donut with a monocle and a sprinkle staff,
  laughs at him (NPC).
- He cries in the rain ("Gary didn't trust agents with his money. It's over.").
- He finds Naïve; a record scratch, then "We're so back."
- His agents script, film and queue while "Gary just approves the spending" (THIS IS SO TUFF).
- "Baron got mogged" ends on a deep-fried MOGGED.

The end card is Gary with "usenaive.ai" and "DEPLOY A COMPANY". Every claim is from the site: pick
a template, deploy it, agents run it, you approve what costs money.

**Weak spots:**
- Veo improvised a sad close-up inside shots 6 and 7, so "Gary stands on business" plays over a
  crying potato.
- One word at a time is the reference's style, but in still frames it reads as fragments; the
  check took points off for it.
- The THIS IS SO TUFF stamp shares the frame with a caption word.

### mobilerun.ai: the aura edit

**Built on** [@yboatkid, "best aura farmer so far"](https://www.tiktok.com/@yboatkid/video/7521629317063822648):
98,878,806 views, 9,092,175 likes, 766,697 shares and 469,033 saves on an account with 88,823
followers (1,113x). It is 19 s and was posted 2025-06-30. Only the format is borrowed: one figure
does nothing, nonchalantly, while everyone behind it works. There is no person, boat race or dance
from the original.

**The video:**
- A chrome robot stands unbothered while a fireball explodes behind it and a car crashes through
  glass into a rooftop pool ("POV: your agent got a phone and 20 more").
- It aura-farms in slow motion while a crew of cartoon phones works behind it (+1000 AURA).
- It mogs a panicking tower of tangled phones ("Bro thinks he needs a rack.").
- It goes deep-fried and fisheye on "Twenty phones. Thirty-one seconds." (6-7). That is
  mobilerun's own "20 tests passed · 31s".
- It walks off into the sunset (STANDING ON BUSINESS).

It is all over bass-boosted phonk with a gravelly deadpan voice. The end card reads
"mobilerun.ai", "DESCRIBE THE TASK, IT RUNS".

**Weak spots:**
- After the opener the story is thin.
- The product (a phone actually doing a task) never shows on screen.
- The end card holds about 5 s on one static robot.

### monid.ai again: a riff on the campaign's own research

This is the one for the demo: a video built on a reference from the Monid campaign's own research
(`research_json` of `cmp_f98819d8b08f46a7a422`). It was found by the competitor search for
Composio.

**Built on** [@phamhacks, "people be so greedy smh"](https://www.tiktok.com/@phamhacks/video/7678518337311116557):
2,276,109 views, 201,565 likes, 11,334 shares and 20,276 saves on an account with 5,088 followers
(447x). It is 55 s and was posted 2026-08-27. It is a paid Composio post (#composiopartner).
- **The original:** a creator emails five million people asking for $1, and a funnel diagram of the
  results fills the top of the screen while he complains about the "greedy" replies.
- **What we took:** its structure and its words (transcribed on Naive for $0.003), never its
  person or its claims.
- **What we left out:** its mass-email premise, which would read as spam on a sponsor's account.

**The video** is called "i let my agent cook with one balance (it got greedy)", 30.6 s, built on the
new `funnel` bit:
- Moni, a glossy black blob like Monid's logo, rides a rocket chair through confetti ("POV: you
  told your agent to go crazy", AURA FARMING).
- Moni reads out its agent's results card one row at a time, with a stamp on each line: 2,900+
  tools it looked at, 12 candidates ranked by fit and price, 0 sign-ups, $0.0013 charged for one
  call, $1 free credit to start.
- It names Monid mid-way ("One skill file. Zero sign-ups.") and ends on "What should my agent do
  next?" and the monid.ai card.

**Cost:** $2.49, with the final check at 7/10, safe. Two script drafts and the transcription
added about $0.30.

**Edited by hand:**
- The writer's lines ran too long for the budget, so I cut each to 11 words or fewer.
- They also named two AI labs from Monid's own brief, which I took out.

The linter now catches both, and the notes in `script.json` say so.

**Weak spots:**
- The video model framed Moni bigger than asked. The card is now at the very top and grows with
  its rows, so it no longer covers the face.
- The voice is Naive's gpt-audio (Monid's wallet was empty), not ElevenLabs.
- The 3-word captions read as fragments in stills.

## What the runs showed, all fixed in the code

- **Monid's wallet ran dry.** Audio now falls back to Naive's own speech, music and sound-effect
  models.
- **Naive refuses more than 12 images in one message.** The clip check now sends six clips at a
  time.
- **The image model drew "a smug chrome robot" as a human-shaped mannequin** with a briefs-like
  gold shape. Every character prompt now says it is not a human figure, and the robot came back
  chunky and mechanical.
- **The critic's safer opener was thrown away.** It swapped a rooftop-ledge parachute for a
  monster-truck stunt, but the linter's stunt list didn't know "monster truck". The list is wider,
  and a critic rewrite is kept unless it breaks a hard rule.
- **Veo only renders 4, 6 or 8 s.** A clip up to 1.35x shorter than its shot now plays slowed down
  instead of paying for the next length.
- **The product's web page made a flat, risky end card.** The final check failed monid.ai's page
  for the row of other companies' logos on it, and called it a flat ending. The end card is now
  the mascot with the site in giant letters.
- **The Italian-accented voice reads 1.5 words a second.** Each bit now has a voice tempo (the
  anthem plays 1.3x faster, pitch kept).

## Not done or not checked

- **Not run live in a campaign:** the director picking `brainrot`, the script room writing it and
  production running it. Those steps are tested with fakes only.
- **Monid's ElevenLabs voices (the Italian accent, the deep story voice) only work while Monid has
  money.** On Naive the voices are OpenAI's (verse, ballad, ash). All three samples kept their
  ElevenLabs voices.
- **Held from posting:** brainrot videos are `internal`, and publishing refuses them until a person
  clears them.

## Make them again

```bash
NOMEN_DATA_DIR=/path/to/nomen/data NOMEN_BRAINROT_LIVE=1 NOMEN_BRAINROT_USD_PER_SECOND=0.08 npx vitest run tests/brainrot.live.test.ts -t monid
```

`NOMEN_BRAINROT_LIVE=script` writes and prints the scripts only, and `probe` also renders the first
clip to price the video model. A rerun resumes from the kept files, and `NOMEN_BRAINROT_RECUT=1`
cuts a finished video again without new renders.
